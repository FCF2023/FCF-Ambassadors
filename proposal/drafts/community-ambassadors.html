Community ambassadors are neighbors who want to welcome anyone new to the Little City and make sure people feel connected. Feeling like you are part of a community has tons of positive impacts on everything from health to governance! If you’d like to be connected to someone or volunteer to become a community ambassador yourself, let us know. Ask us about anything, or join our team — fill out the form below or reach out to us by email.
