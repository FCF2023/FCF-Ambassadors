When a new neighbor reaches out

  1. Say hello within a couple of days. A short, friendly note is plenty.
  2. Ask what they’re looking for. Schools, parks, getting around, meeting people: let them steer.
  3. Point them somewhere useful. The Welcome guide and the Community Directory cover most first questions. Invite them to whatever is next on the calendar.
  4. Stuck? Ask the group. Someone on the team will know, and the answer helps the next ambassador too.
